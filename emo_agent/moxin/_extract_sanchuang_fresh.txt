1.1.1 项目简介
Project Introduction
“墨心智愈”是在原项目“墨韵心调”研究与实践基础上的系统化升级成果，面向青年群体及心理亚健康人群，构建以“国画疗愈+音乐干预+情感大模型”为核心的智能化情绪干预平台项目围绕“情绪识别—内容生成—干预引导—疗效评估—模型迭代”形成可验证、可优化、可复制的闭环体系，实现个体化、日常化、低门槛的数字疗愈服务供给相较于传统单模态心理支持方案，本项目在多模态协同能力、文化适配深度与效果量化机制方面具有显著优势，兼具技术创新价值、应用推广价值与社会公益价值
"Ink Mind Healing" is a systematically upgraded achievement based on the research and practice of the original project "Ink Rhythm Mind Regulation". Targeting young people and people with suboptimal mental health, it constructs an intelligent emotional intervention platform centered on "traditional Chinese painting therapy + music intervention + emotional large model". The project forms a verifiable, optimizable and replicable closed-loop system around "emotion recognition – content generation – intervention guidance – efficacy evaluation – model iteration", realizing the provision of individualized, daily and low-threshold digital healing services. Compared with traditional single-modal psychological support programs, this project has significant advantages in multi-modal collaboration capabilities, depth of cultural adaptation and effect quantification mechanisms, and possesses technological innovation value, application promotion value and social public welfare value.
1.1.2 项目初心
Original intention of the project
“墨韵心调”项目的核心初心，是在严格恪守心理干预科学性、专业性与严谨性的基础上，着力打破传统心理服务的壁垒，有效降低用户获取情绪支持的门槛，持续提升心理服务的可及性、便捷性与体验友好性，让更多有情绪调节需求的用户能够无负担、易接触地获得优质、规范的心理支持
为实现这一初心，项目以中华优秀传统文化中的国画艺术为核心情绪表达媒介，深度挖掘国画笔墨、构图、意境中蕴含的疗愈价值，将其与舒缓适配的音乐元素、智能化交互技术有机融合，构建起一套兼具东方审美特质与现代科技优势的数字疗愈方案，既保留心理干预的科学内核，又赋予服务浓厚的文化底蕴与舒适的体验感，打破传统心理服务的刻板印象
项目聚焦当前社会心理服务领域的核心痛点，着力解决“用户有情绪调节需求但不愿主动求助、有求助意愿但难以长期坚持”的结构性问题，通过轻量化、场景化的服务设计，推动心理支持服务从“问题出现后的被动干预”向“日常化、轻量化、陪伴式的主动调节”延伸，助力用户在日常场景中培养情绪管理能力，实现情绪的自我疏导与正向调节，最终推动全民情绪健康素养的提升
The core original aspiration of the "Ink Rhythm & Mental Regulation" project is, on the basis of strictly abiding by the scientificity, professionalism and rigor of psychological intervention, to strive to break down the barriers of traditional psychological services, effectively lower the threshold for users to access emotional support, continuously improve the accessibility, convenience and user-friendliness of psychological services, and enable more users in need of emotional regulation to obtain high-quality and standardized psychological support without burden and with easy access.
To fulfill this aspiration, the project takes traditional Chinese painting, an integral part of China's outstanding traditional culture, as the core medium for emotional expression. It deeply explores the therapeutic value inherent in the brushwork, composition and artistic conception of traditional Chinese painting, and organically integrates it with soothing and matching musical elements as well as intelligent interactive technologies. This has formed a digital therapy solution that combines both oriental aesthetic characteristics and modern technological advantages. It not only retains the scientific core of psychological intervention, but also endows the service with profound cultural deposits and a comfortable experience, breaking the stereotypical impression of traditional psychological services.
Focusing on the core pain points in the current field of social psychological services, the project strives to address the structural problems that "users have emotional regulation needs but are unwilling to seek help proactively, and those who have the willingness to seek help find it hard to persist for a long time". Through lightweight and scenario-based service design, it promotes the transformation of psychological support services from "passive intervention after problems arise" to "daily, lightweight and companion-oriented active regulation". It helps users cultivate emotional management abilities in daily scenarios, realize self-catharsis and positive regulation of emotions, and ultimately promote the improvement of the national emotional health literacy.

1.情感疗愈
Emotional Healing
抑郁、焦虑等情绪问题已成为现代社会普遍存在的心理健康挑战。据世界卫生组织统计，全球约10亿人正在遭受情绪困扰。新冠疫情后，全球范围内焦虑和抑郁发病率呈上升趋势。情绪问题不仅影响个体的心理健康，还可能对生活、社交及未来发展造成深远影响。如下图 1 所示，抑郁等情绪障碍在全球普遍存在 , 且广泛分布。
Emotional problems such as depression and anxiety have become prevalent mental health challenges in modern society. According to the World Health Organization, approximately 1 billion people worldwide are suffering from emotional distress. Following the COVID-19 pandemic, the incidence of anxiety and depression has shown an upward trend globally. Emotional problems not only affect an individual's mental health but may also exert a profound impact on life, social interactions and future development. 近年来，随着社会节奏的加快、社交压力的增加以及数字化信息过载等外部因素的影响，人们的心理健康问题日益凸显。调查研究发现，早期的心理干预和情绪调节训练可以显著改善个体的情绪稳定性，提高其应对压力的能力，并增强社会适应能力。及时识别和干预不仅有助于减少负面情绪的累积，还能有效提升心理韧性，使其更好地管理自身情绪。然而，由于情绪问题的隐匿性、心理干预资源的不均衡以及社会文化对心理健康的认知偏差，使得情绪障碍的早期筛查和干预面临巨大挑战。
In recent years, influenced by external factors such as the accelerated pace of society, increased social pressure, and digital information overload, people's mental health issues have become increasingly prominent. Research surveys have found that early psychological intervention and emotional regulation training can significantly improve an individual's emotional stability, enhance their ability to cope with stress, and strengthen social adaptability. Timely identification and intervention not only help reduce the accumulation of negative emotions but also effectively boost psychological resilience, enabling individuals to better manage their own emotions. However, the concealed nature of emotional problems, the uneven distribution of psychological intervention resources, and socio-cultural biases in the understanding of mental health pose enormous challenges to the early screening and intervention of emotional disorders.
首先，情绪问题具有高度的个体化和复杂性。其表现形式可能包括情绪低落 、焦虑、易怒、社交退缩等，但这些症状往往难以被察觉，且可能与其他心理状态相混淆，例如学业、工作压力导致的短期情绪波动或青春期、更年期情绪不稳定等。这种复杂性增加了准确识别情绪障碍的难度。而传统的心理评估方法，如问卷调查或心理咨询，可能因受测者的主观表达偏差或评估标准的差异而影响准确性。
First of all, emotional problems are highly individualized and complex. Their manifestations may include low mood, anxiety, irritability, social withdrawal, and so on. However, these symptoms are often difficult to detect and may be confused with other psychological states, such as short-term mood swings caused by academic or work pressure, or emotional instability during adolescence or menopause. This complexity increases the difficulty of accurately identifying emotional disorders. Traditional psychological assessment methods, such as questionnaires or psychological counseling, may affect accuracy due to the subjective expression bias of the respondents or differences in evaluation criteria.
其次，由于心理健康科普的不足，社会对心理问题仍然存在一定程度的，部分人群可能缺乏对情绪障碍的认知，甚至对心理问题持回避态度。此外，心理健康资源的分布不均衡，导致部分地区的人们难以及时获得专业的心理支持。下图

基于此，“ 墨心智愈 ”AI驱动的国画多模态情感疗愈平台通过融合深度学习技术、情感计算与国画艺术表达，为人们提供智能化的情绪筛查及个性化干预方案。本平台不仅能够利用AI识别用户的情感状态，还通过多模态数据（如绘画、语音、表情分析等）精准评估情绪波动，为用户提供基于国画艺术的情感疏导体验，从而助力心理健康的早期干预与自我调节。

The platform can not only use AI to identify users' emotional states, but also accurately assess emotional fluctuations through multi-modal data (such as painting, voice, facial expression analysis, etc.), providing users with emotional counseling experiences based on traditional Chinese painting art, thereby facilitating the early intervention and self-regulation of mental health.
1.1.4 技术背景
Technical Background
(1)语言模型
项目选用EmoLLM作为语言模型。EmoLLM（Emotion Large Language Model）是一种专为情感计算设计的大规模预训练语言模型，融合自然语言处理、多模态情绪识别和深度学习技术，具备对人类情感进行精准感知、解析与生成的能力。相较于传统的大语言模型，EmoLLM 更侧重于情感识别、情绪调节及智能心理干预，广泛应用于情感陪伴、心理健康辅助手段及艺术疗愈等领域。
EmoLLM 基于多模态深度学习架构，通过神经网络融合不同模态的信息，以提高情感识别的精准度和泛化能力。在文本处理方面，它采用Transformer 架构（如 BERT、GPT 或 T5），利用自注意力机制对情感语境进行深度建模，并通过情感嵌入增强对情绪特征的理解。在语音情感识别方面，EmoLLM 结合卷积神经网络与循环神经网络，对音频信号进行时频特征提取，并通过端到端语音情感分析建模长时间序列情感依赖性。此外，在计算机视觉情感识别领域，EmoLLM 采用CNN（如 ResNet、EfficientNet）与视觉变换器相结合的方式，提取面部表情信息，精准分析微表情的情绪特征，并结合时序卷积网络或双向 LSTM追踪面部表情的动态变化。
EmoLLM 还具备情感驱动的智能生成能力，能够根据用户情绪状态动态生成文本、音乐、绘画等艺术作品。在文本生成方面，它采用情感控制的语言建模，结合情感向量调整文本风格，使其具备针对性情绪特征，如安慰、鼓励或共鸣。此外，EmoLLM 结合强化学习优化生成结果，使其更符合用户的情感需求。
在心理健康支持方面，EmoLLM 结合心理学与AI技术，提供个性化心理支持 , 并具备智能情绪疏导功能。作为情感陪伴助手，它能够通过强化学习和长时记忆机制模拟富有情感的对话方式，提供长期的情感陪伴与交互。 同时，EmoLLM结合心理健康量表（如 PHQ-9、GAD-7）进行用户情绪评估，提供科学化的心理调适建议。此外，EmoLLM 采用深度强化学习，根据用户的历史情绪数据优化干预策略，如冥想、艺术疗愈或认知行为疗法（CBT）。通过情绪追踪，EmoLLM 还能够建立个性化情绪档案，为用户提供长期的心理健康管理方案。
Language Model
The project selects EmoLLM as the language model. EmoLLM (Emotion Large Language Model) is a large-scale pre-trained language model designed specifically for affective computing. It integrates natural language processing, multimodal emotion recognition, and deep learning technologies, and possesses the ability to accurately perceive, analyze, and generate human emotions. Compared with traditional large language models, EmoLLM focuses more on emotion recognition, emotion regulation, and intelligent psychological intervention, and is widely used in fields such as emotional companionship, mental health assistance, and art therapy.
Based on a multimodal deep learning architecture, EmoLLM fuses information from different modalities through neural networks to improve the accuracy and generalization ability of emotion recognition. In text processing, it adopts the Transformer architecture (such as BERT, GPT, or T5), uses the self-attention mechanism to deeply model emotional contexts, and enhances the understanding of emotional features through emotion embedding. In speech emotion recognition, EmoLLM combines convolutional neural networks and recurrent neural networks to extract time-frequency features from audio signals, and models long-term sequence emotional dependencies through end-to-end speech emotion analysis. In addition, in the field of computer vision emotion recognition, EmoLLM adopts a combination of CNN (such as ResNet, EfficientNet) and vision transformers to extract facial expression information, accurately analyze emotional features of microexpressions, and track dynamic changes in facial expressions by combining temporal convolutional networks or bidirectional LSTMs.
EmoLLM also has emotion-driven intelligent generation capabilities, enabling it to dynamically generate artistic works such as texts, music, and paintings according to users' emotional states. In text generation, it employs emotion-controlled language modeling and adjusts text styles in combination with emotion vectors to endow texts with targeted emotional characteristics, such as comfort, encouragement, or resonance. Furthermore, EmoLLM optimizes generation results through reinforcement learning to better meet users' emotional needs.
In terms of mental health support, EmoLLM integrates psychology and AI technologies to provide personalized psychological support and features intelligent emotional counseling functions. As an emotional companion assistant, it can simulate emotionally rich dialogue patterns through reinforcement learning and long-term memory mechanisms, providing long-term emotional companionship and interaction. Meanwhile, EmoLLM conducts user emotion assessments in combination with mental health scales  and provides scientific psychological adjustment suggestions. In addition, EmoLLM uses deep reinforcement learning to optimize intervention strategies based on users' historical emotional data, such as meditation, art therapy, or cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT). Through emotion tracking, EmoLLM can also establish personalized emotion profiles and provide users with long-term mental health management plans.

艺术特征感知算法：针对国画核心艺术特征（水墨笔触、留白构图、意境氛围），设计多模态视觉 - 语言对齐损失函数，将东方美学规则嵌入模型训练过程，强化模型对 “情绪→ 国画意象” 的映射能力；
情绪精准度约束：引入情绪标签约束的条件扩散机制，在生成过程中绑定用户情绪强度与艺术风格权重，确保输出内容与用户情绪状态（如焦虑→沉静山水 、低落→ 明快花鸟）高度匹配，解决通用模型 “情绪感知模糊” 的痛点；
泛化能力增强：结合自监督学习与风格数据增强（如笔触扰动、构图变换） , 提升模型在跨情绪、跨风格场景下的鲁棒性，避免生成内容同质化，保障疗愈体验的多样性。

Visual Model
The visual generation module of this project abandons the invocation of general commercial models. Taking the large model Stable Diffusion XL (SDXL) as the foundation, it conducts independent training and domain adaptation of core algorithms for the scenario of Eastern traditional Chinese painting emotional healing, forming visual generation capabilities with fully independent intellectual property rights. The core advantages and implementation are as follows:
Model Foundation and Basis for Algorithm Innovation
SDXL is a benchmark model in the current open-source text-to-image field. Based on the cascaded diffusion architecture and multi-scale self-attention mechanism, it generates images with a resolution of 1024×1024 and above through progressive latent space denoising, achieving industry-leading levels in detail restoration, scene consistency and artistic expressiveness. On this basis, this project carries out customized algorithmic transformation for the segmented demands of Eastern traditional Chinese painting emotional healing, rather than simple invocation or shallow fine-tuning, providing a reliable technical foundation for high-fidelity and high-emotion-adaptability traditional Chinese painting generation.
Core Algorithm Training: Customized Optimization for Eastern Emotion-Traditional Chinese Painting Mapping
Relying on a self-built Eastern traditional Chinese painting emotion dataset (covering classic landscape and flower-and-bird works from the Tang, Song, Yuan, Ming and Qing dynasties, with annotations for 6 groups of Eastern emotional dimensions such as "bold-unconstrained & graceful-restrained" and "serene-quiet & lively-bustling", and a sample size of over 1,000), the team has designed a complete core algorithm training pipeline:
Lightweight fine-tuning strategy: Adopting a training paradigm of joint optimization of LoRA (Low-Rank Adaptation) and prompt engineering, it efficiently injects artistic features and emotional semantics of Eastern traditional Chinese painting while fine-tuning only a small number of parameters (<5% of the total parameters), avoiding catastrophic forgetting of the model;Artistic feature perception algorithm: For the core artistic features of traditional Chinese painting (ink wash brushstrokes, blank-leaving composition, artistic conception atmosphere), a multimodal visual-language alignment loss function is designed to embed Eastern aesthetic rules into the model training process, strengthening the model's mapping ability from "emotions to traditional Chinese painting imagery";
Emotion accuracy constraint: Introducing a conditional diffusion mechanism constrained by emotion labels, binding user emotional intensity and artistic style weights during the generation process to ensure that the output content is highly matched with the user's emotional state (e.g., anxiety → serene landscapes, depression → bright flower-and-bird paintings), solving the pain point of "vague emotion perception" in general models;
Generalization capability enhancement: Combining self-supervised learning and style data augmentation (such as brushstroke perturbation, composition transformation) to improve the model's robustness in cross-emotion and cross-style scenarios, avoid homogenization of generated content, and ensure the diversity of healing experiences.The above training process and model architecture have been visualized (see Figure 3), clearly demonstrating the embedding logic of the LoRA fine-tuning module in the SDXL Base and Refiner models, as well as the complete technical link of "emotional semantics → model training → traditional Chinese painting generation".

1.2 项目背景与意义
Project Background and Significance
1.2.1 国家战略层面的心理健康政策梳理
Review of Mental Health Policies at the National Strategic Level
《“健康中国2030”规划纲要》是心理健康领域的最高层级战略文件，明确将心理健康纳入公共卫生体系重点，提出到2030年居民心理健康素养水平提升至30%的目标
The "Healthy China 2030" Plan Outline is the highest-level strategic document in the field of mental health. It clearly incorporates mental health into the priorities of the public health system and sets a goal to raise the level of residents' mental health literacy to 30% by 2030.​
为落实这一纲要，国家层面在2025-2026年密集推出专项行动：2025年4月，国务院办公厅印发《关于开展“儿科和精神卫生服务年”行动（2025—2027年）的通知》，将心理健康服务向社区、学校、企业等场景延伸，要求2025年县域精神卫生服务网点覆盖率达50%，2027年实现县域线上心理服务全覆盖​；2026年1月，我国首部国家级艺术疗愈领域发展报告——《中国艺术疗愈发展报告蓝皮书》筹委会在京启动，其核心指导单位涵盖国家卫健委、文旅部、教育部相关直属机构，旨在构建艺术疗愈的学科框架与行业标准，为“传统文化+心理服务”提供专业指引与决策支持
To implement this outline, the state has intensively launched special campaigns from 2025 to 2026: in April 2025, the General Office of the State Council issued the Circular on Launching the "Year of Pediatrics and Mental Health Services" Campaign (2025–2027), extending mental health services to communities, schools, enterprises and other scenarios, requiring the coverage rate of county-level mental health service outlets to reach 50% in 2025 and achieve full coverage of county-level online psychological services in 2027; in January 2026, the organizing committee of China Art Therapy Development Report Blue Book, China's first national-level development report in the field of art therapy, was launched in Beijing. Its core guiding authorities include relevant directly affiliated institutions of the National Health Commission, the Ministry of Culture and Tourism, and the Ministry of Education, aiming to build a disciplinary framework and industry standards for art therapy, and provide professional guidance and decision-making support for the integration of traditional culture and psychological services.
从政策导向的演变看，2025-2026年的核心变化是从“体系建设”转向“服务深化”：此前政策的重点是“有没有”——比如是否建立心理援助热线、是否配备专职心理教师；而本轮政策的重点是“好不好”——比如服务是否贴合人群需求、是否能通过非药物手段实现更温和的干预其中，《蓝皮书》的启动是政策细化的里程碑事件：它首次将艺术疗愈从“辅助手段”定位为“心理健康服务体系的有机组成部分”，为“墨韵心调”这类以书法等传统艺术为载体的项目，提供了国家级的专业认可框架
From the perspective of the evolution of policy orientation, the core change in 2025–2026 is the shift from "system construction" to "service deepening": previously, policies focused on "availability" — for instance, whether psychological assistance hotlines had been established and whether full-time psychological teachers had been assigned; while the current round of policies emphasizes "quality" — such as whether services meet the needs of target groups and whether milder interventions can be achieved through non-pharmaceutical means. Among these, the launch of the Blue Book marks a milestone in policy refinement: it for the first time positions art therapy as an "integral part of the mental health service system" rather than an "auxiliary method", providing a national-level professional recognition framework for programs like "Moyun Xindiao" that take traditional arts such as calligraphy as their carriers.
2025年是《全国社会心理服务体系建设试点工作方案》的终期验收年，试点地区的成果直接推动了全国层面的政策升级从验收数据看，试点地区的核心指标均超额完成：全国333个地级市、直辖市的87个区县已全部完成心理门诊、睡眠门诊设置，心理门诊服务量较2024年同期提升17%，睡眠门诊服务量提升39%——这两项数据是政策落地成效的直接验证，也反映出群众对专业心理健康服务的需求正在快速释放​
2025 marks the final acceptance year of the Pilot Work Plan for the Construction of a National Social Psychological Service System. The achievements of pilot areas have directly driven the upgrading of national-level policies. Judging from the acceptance data, all core indicators in pilot areas have been exceeded: 87 districts and counties under 333 prefecture-level cities and municipalities directly under the Central Government across the country have all set up psychological clinics and sleep clinics. The service volume of psychological clinics increased by 17% compared with the same period in 2024, and that of sleep clinics rose by 39%. These two sets of data serve as direct proof of the effective implementation of policies and also reflect the rapidly growing demand of the public for professional mental health services.
试点工作的核心价值，在于探索出了可复制的“非药物干预”落地模式：比如部分试点城市将书法、音乐等艺术形式纳入社区心理健康服务包，针对轻度焦虑、抑郁群体开展定期干预；还有城市依托医院心理门诊，为患者提供“医疗咨询+艺术疗愈”的组合服务——这些实践不仅验证了非药物干预的有效性，更为后续政策的细化提供了参考案例​
The core value of the pilot work lies in exploring a replicable implementation model of "non-pharmacological interventions". For instance, some pilot cities have incorporated artistic forms such as calligraphy and music into community mental health service packages to provide regular interventions for groups with mild anxiety and depression. Other cities, relying on psychological outpatient clinics in hospitals, offer patients a combined service of "medical consultation plus art therapy". These practices have not only verified the effectiveness of non-pharmacological interventions but also provided reference cases for the refinement of follow-up policies.
2025年是《全国社会心理服务体系建设试点工作方案》的终期验收年，试点地区的成果直接推动了全国层面的政策升级从验收数据看，试点地区的核心指标均超额完成：全国333个地级市、直辖市的87个区县已全部完成心理门诊、睡眠门诊设置，心理门诊服务量较2024年同期提升17%，睡眠门诊服务量提升39%——这两项数据是政策落地成效的直接验证，也反映出群众对专业心理健康服务的需求正在快速释放​

These practices have not only verified the effectiveness of non-pharmacological interventions but also provided reference cases for the refinement of subsequent policies.
1.2.2 部门规章与跨部门协同政策
Departmental Rules and Cross-Departmental Collaborative Policies
教育部是学生心理健康工作的牵头部门，其政策体系围绕“全流程防护、全场景覆盖”构建2023年4月发布的《全面加强和改进新时代学生心理健康工作专项行动计划（2023—2025年）》明确了量化指标：到2025年，配备专（兼）职心理健康教育教师的学校比例达到95%，开展心理健康教育的家庭教育指导服务站点比例达到60%，高校专职心理咨询师师生比不低于1:4000且每校至少配备2名​
2025年10月印发的《进一步加强中小学生心理健康工作十条措施》，则是对专项计划的细化与强化：文件不再仅停留在“队伍建设”层面，而是从减轻学业负担、保障睡眠等基础生理需求入手，系统性构建“健康教育-监测预警-咨询服务-干预处置”四位一体的工作体系——比如要求“每所学校至少配备1名专（兼）职心理教师”“建立学生心理问题线上线下联动发现机制”，同时明确将心理健康教育纳入学校考核指标，确保政策落地不打折扣值得注意的是，教育部的政策导向已从“危机干预”转向“积极心理建设”：2026年政府工作报告明确将美育与心理健康教育融合，提出“艺术教育不再是技能训练，而是情感教育、人格养成的重要途径”——这意味着书法、绘画等传统艺术形式，将正式成为学生心理健康教育的重要载体，而非此前的“课外活动”​
The Ministry of Education is the leading department responsible for students' mental health work, and its policy system is built around the principles of "whole-process protection and all-scenario coverage". The Special Action Plan for Comprehensively Strengthening and Improving Students' Mental Health Work in the New Era (2023–2025), issued in April 2023, set clear quantitative targets: by 2025, the proportion of schools equipped with full-time or part-time mental health education teachers shall reach 95%, the proportion of family education guidance service stations offering mental health education shall reach 60%, and the ratio of full-time psychological counselors to students in institutions of higher education shall be no lower than 1:4,000, with at least two such counselors per university.
The Ten Measures to Further Strengthen Mental Health Work Among Primary and Secondary School Students, issued in October 2025, refine and strengthen the special action plan. Rather than focusing only on "team building", the document starts with meeting basic physiological needs such as reducing academic burdens and ensuring sleep, and systematically establishes a four-in-one work system of "health education, monitoring and early warning, counseling services, and intervention and handling". For instance, it requires "each school to be equipped with at least one full-time or part-time psychological teacher" and "to establish an online-offline linked mechanism for detecting students' psychological problems". Meanwhile, it explicitly incorporates mental health education into school assessment indicators to ensure full policy implementation.
Notably, the Ministry of Education's policy orientation has shifted from "crisis intervention" to "positive psychological development". The 2026 Government Work Report clearly integrates aesthetic education with mental health education, stating that "art education is no longer merely skill training but an important approach to emotional education and personality cultivation". This means traditional art forms such as calligraphy and painting will officially become important carriers of students' mental health education, rather than being treated as "extracurricular activities" as before.

Target group alignment: The project's core service groups (university students and young working professionals) are precisely the high-need groups prioritized by policies — the Ministry of Education identifies university students as the core target of mental health work, and the All-China Federation of Trade Unions also includes young working professionals in its key service scope.

1.3.1 政策可行性
Policy feasibility
项目方向与国家“健康中国2030”战略、心理健康服务体系建设以及数字技术赋能公共服务的政策导向高度一致高校、企业与社区场景对心理支持服务需求持续提升，为项目试点与推广提供了明确政策环境“墨韵心调”采用非医疗化、陪伴式、轻干预路径，能够在合规前提下补充传统心理服务供给，具备较强的政策适配性与社会协同基础

Market feasibility
目标用户（高校学生、青年职场群体、心理亚健康人群）规模大、需求真实且具备持续性传统线下服务在覆盖率、成本与使用频率方面存在限制，数字化情绪调节产品具有明显替代与补充价值项目以“文化疗愈+AI个性化”切入差异化赛道，既能满足用户情绪调节需求，又能通过校园与企业渠道实现规模化触达，市场落地路径明确
The target user groups (university students, young professionals, and people with suboptimal mental health) are large in scale, with genuine and sustained needs. Traditional offline services are limited in terms of coverage, cost, and frequency of use, making digital emotional regulation products of significant alternative and supplementary value. By entering a differentiated market segment with a focus on "cultural healing + AI personalization", the project not only meets users' emotional regulation needs but also achieves large-scale outreach through campus and corporate channels, with a clear market implementation path.
1.3.3 技术可行性
Technical Feasibility
项目技术路线清晰，采用“情绪识别—内容生成—干预引导—疗效评估—模型迭代”闭环架构，具备工程实现可操作性在模型能力方面，可基于现有成熟大模型能力进行二次开发；在系统层面，通过模块化设计实现识别、生成、评估各环节解耦，便于迭代升级；在验证层面，可通过量表与行为数据双维度评估干预效果，形成可复现实证链路
The project features a clear technical route, adopting a closed-loop architecture of "emotion recognition—content generation—intervention guidance—efficacy evaluation—model iteration", which is feasible for engineering implementation. In terms of model capabilities, secondary development can be carried out based on the capabilities of existing mature large models; at the system level, modular design is adopted to decouple the links of recognition, generation and evaluation, facilitating iterative upgrades; at the verification level, intervention effects can be assessed from both scale and behavioral data dimensions, forming a repeatable empirical chain.
1.3.4 商业可行性
Commercial Feasibility
项目可采用“B端场景落地+C端持续服务”的双轮驱动模式前期通过高校心理中心、企业EAP等合作场景完成试点验证，中期依托标准化产品与服务流程实现复制扩张，后期形成订阅、服务包、联合内容合作等复合收益结构随着用户规模增长，平台边际服务成本下降，具备由验证期向稳态运营期过渡的商业可行性
The project can adopt a two-wheel drive model of "B-side scenario implementation + C-side continuous service". In the early stage, pilot verification will be completed through cooperative scenarios such as university psychological centers and corporate EAPs. In the medium term, replication and expansion will be achieved relying on standardized products and service processes. In the later stage, a composite revenue structure will be formed, including subscriptions, service packages, and joint content cooperation. With the growth of user scale, the platform's marginal service cost will decrease, which makes it commercially feasible to transition from the verification period to the steady-state operation period.

Market Analysis

The system quantitatively evaluates intervention effects through user feedback, behavioral data and phased scale results, and returns valid samples to model training to continuously optimize the quality of strategy recommendation and content generation. 核心研究目标
Core research objectives
本项目聚焦多模态情感计算与东方艺术美学的交叉创新，以国画为核心载体，围绕 “情绪支持 ” 与 “ 共感交互 ” 两大核心目标，搭建系统化东方审美情绪坐标体系，研发可解释的东方艺术情绪表达模型，构建全维度多模态情绪支持体系。
在心理健康领域，EmoVision平台通过多模态技术整合对患者进行多感官干预治疗。患者通过与EmoLLM模型进行多轮对话交流，通过文字或语音描述自身状态，emollm分析识别患者潜在心理问题，对患者相对欠缺的情感进行强化。
基于情感诊断结果，大模型将EmoVision平台与传统文化结合进行干预。 EmoLLM从标注情绪的国画数据库中匹配作品用于情感治疗，通过ai实时生成新的国画作品，更新数据集用于治疗。
治疗过程中，视听文三者结合。生成的情绪文本、 国画画面与音乐相符，多感官协同机制，极大的增强了治疗效果。
This project focuses on the interdisciplinary innovation of multimodal affective computing and Oriental artistic aesthetics. With traditional Chinese painting as the core carrier, it centers on the two core goals of "emotional support" and "empathic interaction", establishes a systematic Oriental aesthetic emotional coordinate system, develops an interpretable emotional expression model for Oriental art, and constructs a comprehensive multimodal emotional support system.
In the field of mental health, the EmoVision platform provides multi-sensory intervention therapy for patients through the integration of multimodal technologies. Patients communicate with the EmoLLM model through multiple rounds of dialogue, describing their conditions via text or voice. EmoLLM analyzes and identifies potential psychological problems of patients and strengthens the emotions that patients are relatively deficient in.
Based on the results of emotional diagnosis, the large model integrates the EmoVision platform with traditional culture for intervention. EmoLLM matches works from an emotion-annotated traditional Chinese painting database for emotional therapy, generates new traditional Chinese paintings in real time through AI, and updates the dataset for therapeutic use.
During treatment, audio, visual and textual elements are combined. The generated emotional text, traditional Chinese painting images and music are consistent with each other, and the multi-sensory synergy mechanism greatly enhances the therapeutic effect.
核心研究内容
Core research content

Multimodal Fusion-Based Emotional Diagnosis and In-Depth Analysis of Emotional Needs
This project relies on the EmoLLM large model to build a three-dimensional multimodal emotion perception system integrating text, speech and facial microexpressions, breaking through the limitations of single interaction forms. Through multiple rounds of intelligent conversations with users and comprehensive analysis of multimodal data, it accurately identifies users' potential emotional problems, deeply parses their emotional need tendencies, extracts target emotional dimensions that require enhancement, and establishes personalized emotional profiles for users, forming scientific and accurate emotional diagnosis results that provide a core basis for subsequent personalized emotional support.

（2） Construct a systematic coordinate system of Oriental aesthetic emotions, build a traditional Chinese painting database with complete annotations of emotional dimensions and artistic characteristics, and establish interpretable mapping rules between emotional features and core aesthetic elements of traditional Chinese paintings such as color, brushstrokes, composition and imagery. Based on emotional diagnosis results, the EmoLLM large model intelligently selects highly compatible traditional Chinese painting works from the database; video flow dynamic technology is introduced to realize scene-based and immersive presentation of static traditional Chinese paintings. If the matching degree of existing works fails to meet expectations, personalized traditional Chinese painting works that fit users' emotional needs will be generated in real time through an AI vision model, and high-quality generated content will be synchronously updated to the database, forming a virtuous cycle of "data matching - content generation - database iteration", and realizing the interpretability and dynamization of emotional expression in Oriental art.

打造 “视觉 - 听觉 - 文本 ” 三位一体的多模态情绪支持体系，确保各维度内容的情绪特征高度统一、协同赋能。视觉层面，以匹配或 AI 生成的国画作品为核心，通过动态化呈现强化情绪视觉传导；听觉层面，依托 AI 音频模型生成与国画情绪意境高度契合的背景音乐，实现音画同步的情绪渲染；文本层面，根据用户情感需求生成针对性情绪引导文本，通过智能交互实现正向情感疏导。借助多感官协同干预机制，强化情绪支持效果，实现从单一模态反馈到多模态深度协同情绪支持的升级。
Construction of a Multimodal Emotional Support Mechanism with Deep Collaboration of Audio, Visual and Textual Content
Establish a trinity multimodal emotional support system integrating "vision, audio and text" to ensure that the emotional characteristics of content in each dimension are highly unified and empower collaboratively. At the visual level, with traditional Chinese paintings matched or AI-generated as the core, emotional visual transmission is enhanced through dynamic presentation; at the audio level, background music highly consistent with the emotional artistic conception of traditional Chinese paintings is generated relying on AI audio models, realizing emotional rendering with synchronized sound and images; at the textual level, targeted emotional guidance texts are generated according to users' emotional needs, and positive emotional counseling is achieved through intelligent interaction. With the multi-sensory collaborative intervention mechanism, the effect of emotional support is strengthened, realizing the upgrading from single-modal feedback to deep multimodal collaborative emotional support.

Design and Development of Group Empathetic Interaction Scenarios.
Breaking through the limitations of individualized personal experience, design a group empathetic interaction system adapted to three core scenarios: campus psychological communities, community art therapy, and online emotional support groups. Build a group emotion visualization module that intuitively presents the emotional states of group members through Eastern artistic elements, enabling mutual perception of group emotions; develop empathetic creative interaction functions that support collaborative traditional Chinese painting creation by multiple people and collective traditional Chinese painting generation based on the overall emotional characteristics of the group, realizing emotional transmission and resonance through creative interaction; construct an art-themed empathetic communication module that guides group members in emotional expression and mutual support combined with traditional Chinese painting content, achieving a breakthrough from individual emotional counseling to group empathetic interaction and large-scale healing.

核心技术模块实现
Implementation of core technical modules
针对上述多模态情绪支持机制与各研究内容的技术落地需求，本项目围绕“东方国画情绪疗愈 ” 目标，构建 “情绪识别→ 多模态生成→协同输出 ” 的闭环技术体系，核心模块实现如下：
In response to the technical implementation requirements of the above multimodal emotion support mechanisms and various research contents, this project focuses on the goal of "emotional healing through traditional Chinese oriental paintings" and constructs a closed-loop technical system of "emotion recognition → multimodal generation → collaborative output". The core modules are implemented as follows:
视觉生成模块以本地化部署 Stable Diffusion WebUI（txt2img API ) 为核心实现，可兼容阿里云通义万相等云端接 口，通过国画风格提示词工程与模型微调，生成匹配用户情绪的东方美学视觉内容。模块优先采用开源模型保障部署灵活性，同时预留云端接口扩展能力，适配不同场景下的生成效果与算力需求。
The visual generation module is mainly implemented based on the locally deployed Stable Diffusion WebUI (txt2img API), and is compatible with cloud interfaces such as Alibaba Cloud Tongyi Wanxiang. Through traditional Chinese painting-style prompt engineering and model fine-tuning, it generates Oriental aesthetic visual content that matches user emotions. The module prioritizes the use of open-source models to ensure deployment flexibility, while reserving cloud interface expansion capabilities to adapt to generation effects and computing power requirements in different scenarios.
音频生成模块采用火山引擎 BGM 生成接 口（如 GenBGMForTime）实现情绪适配背景音乐生成，对话与语音合成模块基于豆包大模型实现。音频内容将根据情绪强度与类型（如舒缓、沉静、开阔等东方情绪维度） 自动匹配节奏与音色，为用户提供沉浸式疗愈听觉体验；对话模块可支持用户情绪倾诉与反馈，强化交互性与共情效果。
The audio generation module adopts the Volcano Engine BGM generation interface (such as GenBGMForTime) to generate emotionally adaptive background music. The dialogue and speech synthesis module is implemented based on the Doubao large model. Audio content will automatically match rhythm and timbre according to emotional intensity and type (such as soothing, calm, open and other oriental emotional dimensions), providing users with an immersive healing auditory experience; the dialogue module can support users' emotional expression and feedback, enhancing interactivity and empathy.
情绪识别与联动模块基于 EmoLLM 完成用户文本输入 → JSON 情绪标签 / 强度输出 → 与 情绪映射配置文件（emotion_to_prompt.csv）联动，自动生成适配的文生图与音乐生成提示词，驱动后续多模态内容生成。该模块实现了 “用户情绪→技术指令 ” 的标准化转换，避免人工干预，保障疗愈内容与用户情绪的精准匹配。
The emotion recognition and linkage module, based on EmoLLM, processes user text input → outputs JSON emotion labels/intensity → links with the emotion mapping configuration file (emotion_to_prompt.csv) to automatically generate suitable text-to-image and music generation prompts, driving subsequent multimodal content generation. This module realizes the standardized conversion of "user emotion → technical instructions", eliminates manual intervention, and ensures the precise matching of healing content with user emotions.
多模态协同机制通过 payload 统一归档、情绪模板选择、共情文案生成、音画同步导出、运行日志（如系统测试日志smoke_test_log.csv）记录等工程化手段，实现文本情绪、视觉风格与音频氛围的高度协同。所有生成内容将关联同一情绪标签，确保疗愈体验的一致性与可控性，同时日志数据可用于后续模型优化与疗效分析。
The multimodal collaboration mechanism achieves high coordination among textual emotions, visual styles, and audio atmospheres through engineering methods such as unified archiving of payloads, selection of emotional templates, generation of empathetic copy, synchronized audio-visual export, and recording of operation logs (e.g., system test logs smoke_test_log.csv). All generated content will be associated with the same emotional tag to ensure the consistency and controllability of the healing experience. Meanwhile, the log data can be used for subsequent model optimization and curative effect analysis.

情绪识别驱动的个体化干预
Individualized Intervention Driven by Emotion Recognition
系统会先判断“你现在的情绪状态是什么”，再决定“下一步该用什么方式帮你调节”
举例来说：同样是压力大，有的人更适合放松型音乐，有的人更需要安静风格的国画引导系统不会“一刀切”，而是根据用户状态动态匹配干预内容

The system will first determine "what your current emotional state is" and then decide "what method should be used to help you regulate it next".
For example: when facing the same high pressure, some people are more suitable for relaxing music, while others need guidance from quiet-style traditional Chinese paintings. The system does not adopt a one-size-fits-all approach; instead, it dynamically matches intervention content according to the user's state.
This makes interventions more precise and also makes it easier for users to stick to using the system.

Multimodal Collaboration of Traditional Chinese Painting and Music

这种方式比单一模态更容易形成连续体验，对缓解焦虑、改善情绪波动更有帮助
The project does not provide a single image or a piece of music independently; instead, it integrates visual and auditory elements into the same intervention process for coordinated output.To put it simply: the system ensures that the "visual scenes seen" and the "music heard" align in emotional orientation—for instance, both leaning towards soothing, stabilizing, or encouraging tones—thereby enhancing immersion and emotional resonance.This approach facilitates a more continuous experience compared to a single modality, making it more effective in alleviating anxiety and improving emotional fluctuations.

Operational risks
经营性风险由项目内部运营、用户管理、资源保障等因素引发，可通过优化管理、调整策略有效防控，是项目运营期的核心管控风险
①用户留存风险
项目核心用户为高校学生、青年职场人等年轻群体，该类用户对轻量化工具新鲜感易消退，对产品交互体验、干预效果、长期价值要求较高若产品交互设计繁琐、情绪干预效果不明显、陪伴感不足，易出现用户活跃度低、7日/30日留存率不佳、用户流失严重等问题，难以形成稳定用户群体
②效果验证风险
心理干预领域暂无全国统一的量化评估标准，行业多依赖主观感受判断效果本项目面向高校、企业等B端机构推广时，合作方对产品干预有效性、数据可信度、落地价值认可度不足，缺乏标准化验证体系，将直接影响项目规模化复制与商业合作推进
③数据安全风险
项目采集、存储、应用用户情绪状态、心理测评结果、交互行为等高度敏感数据，若数据防护措施不到位，可能出现数据泄露、滥用、未授权访问等问题，既违反数据合规要求，也会引发用户信任危机，对项目品牌与口碑造成不可逆损害
④资源约束风险
项目以大学生创业团队为核心，前期存在资金、人力、渠道三重资源约束：研发资金有限，难以支撑大规模技术迭代与市场推广；团队人力不足，研发、运营、推广工作难以全面铺开；校外渠道资源有限，B端合作对接效率较低，阶段性运营压力较大
⑤落地适配风险
高校、企业、社区等核心落地场景的需求存在显著差异，高校侧重学生心理健康普查与日常疏导，企业侧重员工压力管理与EAP服务，社区侧重普惠性心理关怀若项目采用标准化产品方案，未针对不同场景定制化适配，将导致试点落地效果不佳、复制推广效率低下
Operational risks arise from factors such as internal project operation, user management, and resource guarantee. They can be effectively prevented and controlled through optimized management and adjusted strategies, and are the core risks to be managed and controlled during the project operation period.
User retention risk
The core users of the project are young groups such as college students and young professionals. Such users tend to lose interest quickly in lightweight tools and have high requirements for product interaction experience, intervention effects, and long-term value. If the product interaction design is cumbersome, the emotional intervention effect is not obvious, and the sense of companionship is insufficient, problems such as low user activity, poor 7-day/30-day retention rates, and severe user churn are likely to occur, making it difficult to form a stable user base.
Effect verification risk
There is currently no unified national quantitative evaluation standard in the field of psychological intervention, and the industry mostly relies on subjective feelings to judge effects. When the project is promoted to B-end institutions such as universities and enterprises, insufficient recognition from partners regarding the effectiveness of product intervention, data credibility, and implementation value, coupled with the lack of a standardized verification system, will directly affect the large-scale replication of the project and the advancement of commercial cooperation.
Data security risk
The project collects, stores, and applies highly sensitive data such as users' emotional states, psychological assessment results, and interactive behaviors. Inadequate data protection measures may lead to data leakage, abuse, unauthorized access, and other issues. This will not only violate data compliance requirements but also trigger a user trust crisis, causing irreversible damage to the project's brand and reputation.
Resource constraint risk
The project is centered on a college student entrepreneurship team, with triple resource constraints in the early stage: capital, manpower, and channels. Limited R&D funds make it difficult to support large-scale technological iteration and market promotion; insufficient team manpower prevents the full rollout of R&D, operation, and promotion work; limited off-campus channel resources lead to low efficiency in docking B-end cooperation, resulting in considerable phased operational pressure.
Implementation adaptation risk
There are significant differences in demand among core implementation scenarios such as universities, enterprises, and communities. Universities focus on college students' mental health screening and daily counseling, enterprises prioritize employee stress management and EAP services, and communities emphasize inclusive psychological care. If the project adopts a standardized product solution without customized adaptation for different scenarios, it will result in poor pilot implementation effects and low efficiency in replication and promotion.
4.1.2 风险应对措施
Risk response measures
针对上述各类风险，项目建立全周期风险防控体系，遵循“预防为主、精准应对、快速迭代”原则，制定一对一可落地应对措施，最大限度降低风险影响
In response to the various risks mentioned above, the project has established a full-cycle risk prevention and control system, adhering to the principles of "prevention first, precise response, and rapid iteration". It has developed one-to-one implementable countermeasures to minimize the impact of risks to the greatest extent.
系统性风险应对
Systemic Risk Response
①政策监管风险应对
严格遵守国家数据安全、心理健康服务相关法律法规，建立全流程合规管控机制：实行用户数据脱敏处理、授权采集、分级存储、日志审计，全程留痕可追溯；明确产品非医疗定位，不开展疾病诊断、治疗等超出范围的服务，建立高风险心理问题预警与专业机构转介机制；主动对接高校心理健康中心、行业协会，提前获取政策导向，确保产品合规上线、场景合规推广
②市场竞争风险应对
强化“国画疗愈+音乐干预+情感大模型”多模态差异化壁垒，沉淀专属“国画-情感”数据集、干预算法、疗效评估体系，形成不可复制的核心技术与内容优势；聚焦高校、企业两大核心场景深耕，打造标杆试点案例，以口碑带动合作拓展；突出东方文化适配性，区别于西方模式心理产品，提升用户文化认同感与品牌忠诚度
③技术迭代风险应对
采用模块化、解耦式技术架构设计，情绪识别、多模态生成、疗效评估等核心模块可独立升级、快速替换；持续对接心理学专家、AI技术导师，跟踪行业前沿技术，定期优化干预策略与内容生成效果；建立“技术研发-场景验证-反馈迭代”闭环，保障技术方案始终贴合用户需求与行业水平
Response to Policy and Regulatory Risks
Strictly comply with national laws and regulations concerning data security and mental health services, and establish a full-process compliance management and control mechanism: implement user data desensitization, authorized collection, hierarchical storage, and log auditing, with full-process traceable records; clearly define the non-medical positioning of the product, refrain from providing services beyond the scope such as disease diagnosis and treatment, and establish an early warning mechanism for high-risk psychological issues and a referral mechanism to professional institutions; proactively connect with university mental health centers and industry associations to obtain policy guidance in advance, ensuring compliant product launch and scenario-based promotion.
Response to Market Competition Risks
Strengthen the multi-modal differentiated barrier of "traditional Chinese painting therapy + music intervention + emotional large model", accumulate exclusive "traditional Chinese painting-emotion" datasets, intervention algorithms, and curative effect evaluation systems, forming irreplicable core technological and content advantages; focus on in-depth development of the two core scenarios of universities and enterprises, build benchmark pilot cases, and drive partnership expansion through word-of-mouth; highlight the adaptability of oriental culture, differentiate from Western-style psychological products, and enhance users' cultural identity and brand loyalty.
Response to Technological Iteration Risks
Adopt a modular and decoupled technical architecture design, allowing core modules such as emotion recognition, multi-modal generation, and curative effect evaluation to be independently upgraded and quickly replaced; continuously connect with psychological experts and AI technical advisors, track cutting-edge industry technologies, and regularly optimize intervention strategies and content generation effects; establish a closed loop of "technology R&D - scenario verification - feedback iteration" to ensure that technical solutions always align with user needs and industry standards.
经营性风险应对
Response to operational risks
①用户留存风险应对
以“低门槛触达—高频轻交互—持续陪伴服务”为核心运营理念，简化产品交互流程，打造轻量化、沉浸式使用体验；建立用户反馈闭环，通过问卷、社群、使用数据快速收集需求，迭代产品功能；推出阶段性情绪追踪、疗愈报告、个性化计划等增值服务，强化用户长期使用价值，提升粘性与留存率
②效果验证风险应对
构建双维度量化评估体系，结合GAD-7、PHQ-9等专业心理量表与用户行为数据、主观满意度，形成可量化、可复现的干预效果报告；联合高校心理学专业、心理健康中心开展实证研究，发布权威效果验证报告，提升B端合作方认可度；标准化效果验证流程，为规模化推广提供数据支撑
③数据安全风险应对
搭建全方位数据安全防护体系，采用加密存储、访问权限管控、隐私计算等技术，防范数据泄露与滥用；制定数据安全管理制度，明确团队成员数据使用规范，定期开展安全自查；主动符合数据合规要求，签署用户隐私授权协议，保障用户数据权益，筑牢信任基础
④资源约束风险应对
坚持“试点—复制—规模化”渐进式推进策略，控制前期资金与人力投入，优先落地需求明确、资源易对接的高校试点；依托高校导师资源、校企合作平台、创新创业政策，争取资金支持、技术指导与渠道对接；优化团队分工，聚焦核心工作，非核心环节寻求校外合作，降低运营成本
⑤落地适配风险应对
建立场景化定制适配流程，针对高校、企业、社区不同需求，推出标准化+定制化服务包，快速响应场景适配要求；沉淀试点场景的运营经验，形成可复制的落地手册、操作规范，提升多场景推广效率；提前对接场景负责人，精准把握需求痛点，确保产品落地即适配、落地即见效
Response to User Retention Risks
Adopt the core operation philosophy of "low-threshold access – high-frequency light interaction – continuous accompanying services", simplify product interaction processes, and create a lightweight and immersive user experience; establish a closed-loop user feedback mechanism to quickly collect demands through questionnaires, communities, and usage data, and iterate product functions; launch value-added services such as phased emotional tracking, healing reports, and personalized plans to enhance users' long-term usage value, and improve stickiness and retention rates.
Response to Effect Verification Risks
Build a two-dimensional quantitative evaluation system that integrates professional psychological scales such as GAD-7 and PHQ-9 with user behavior data and subjective satisfaction to form quantifiable and reproducible intervention effect reports; conduct empirical research in cooperation with university psychology departments and mental health centers, and issue authoritative effect verification reports to increase recognition from B-side partners; standardize the effect verification process to provide data support for large-scale promotion.
Response to Data Security Risks
Establish a comprehensive data security protection system using technologies such as encrypted storage, access permission control, and privacy computing to prevent data leakage and abuse; formulate data security management systems, clarify data usage specifications for team members, and conduct regular self-inspections on security; proactively comply with data compliance requirements, sign user privacy authorization agreements, protect users' data rights and interests, and consolidate the foundation of trust.
Response to Resource Constraint Risks
Adhere to the progressive promotion strategy of "pilot – replication – scaling up" to control early capital and human input, and prioritize the launch of pilots in universities with clear demands and easy resource connection; rely on university tutor resources, university-enterprise cooperation platforms, and innovation and entrepreneurship policies to secure financial support, technical guidance, and channel connection; optimize team division of labor, focus on core work, and seek off-campus cooperation for non-core links to reduce operating costs.
Response to Implementation Adaptation Risks
Establish a scenario-based customized adaptation process, launch standardized plus customized service packages for different needs of universities, enterprises, and communities to quickly respond to scenario adaptation requirements; summarize operation experience from pilot scenarios to form replicable implementation manuals and operation specifications, improving multi-scenario promotion efficiency; connect with scenario principals in advance to accurately grasp demand pain points, ensuring that products are adapted and effective upon implementation.

Conduct special training on restoring scenes in the traditional Chinese painting style; by learning the brushstroke and ink charm characteristics of classic traditional Chinese paintings such as Six Steeds by Xu Beihong and Dwelling in the Fuchun Mountains by Huang Gongwang, the style restoration accuracy reaches 92%, enabling the generation of customized traditional Chinese painting content that conforms to Oriental aesthetic standards.

For art therapy scenarios, it supports dynamically adjusting music parameters based on the user's real-time status (such as respiratory rate, speech rhythm, and GAD-7 scale score); for example, when the user's respiratory rate exceeds 18 breaths per minute, the model will automatically slow down the music tempo to 60–80 BPM and extend the pause time between phrases to help the user calm their emotions.
